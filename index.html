THE RSHQ APPROACH

Don't just fight the habit. Understand the system behind it.

Awareness creates choice. Repetition creates change.

MAYBE THIS IS YOU

You are not lazy.
You may be running an old pattern.

You have probably told yourself things like:

“From tomorrow, I'll be disciplined.”
“I know what I need to do.”
“Why do I keep doing this?”
“I start strongly, but I can't stay consistent.”

The frustrating part is that knowing is not the same as changing.

Our behaviour is shaped by triggers, beliefs, emotional associations, environment and repetition. If those mechanisms remain unchanged, willpower eventually gets tired.

One-to-one coaching gives you a space to slow down, understand what is happening and turn insight into practical action.

THE PROBLEMS WE WORK ON

What keeps repeating in your life that you don't want to repeat?

01 — HABITS

“I know it's bad for me. I still do it.”

Understand triggers and design a practical interruption instead of relying only on willpower.

02 — DISCIPLINE

“I start. Then I lose momentum.”

Build systems that make consistency easier and reduce dependence on motivation.

03 — OVERTHINKING

“My mind keeps going in circles.”

Notice repetitive thought loops and move from rumination toward deliberate action.

04 — SELF-SABOTAGE

“Why do I get in my own way?”

Explore beliefs and patterns that may be quietly conflicting with your goals.

05 — DIRECTION

“I want change, but I don't know where to begin.”

Turn a vague desire for change into a clear next step and practical experiment.

06 — IDENTITY

“I want to become a different version of myself.”

Translate the person you want to become into behaviours you can practise today.

THE RSHQ METHOD

Insight is the beginning.
Practice creates change.

Sessions focus on understanding the pattern and designing small, realistic actions you can carry into your actual life.

01 — NOTICE

02 — UNDERSTAND
